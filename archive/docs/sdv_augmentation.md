# SDV Synthetic Data Augmentation & Quality Evaluation Report

## 1. Executive Summary & Research Context
- **Research Title:** *An Explainable Policy-Aware Agentic AI Decision Support System for Bank Loan Evaluation Using Machine Learning Predictions*
- **Augmentation Phase:** Step 2.5 — Tabular Synthetic Data Augmentation via Synthetic Data Vault (SDV)
- **Primary Seed Dataset:** 500 validated Google Form survey responses (`data/raw/google_form_responses_500.csv`)
- **Synthetic Generator:** Conditional Tabular Generative Adversarial Network (**CTGAN Synthesizer**) via the official `sdv` library
- **Generated Records:** 2,500 synthetic records (`data/synthetic/sdv_generated_2500.csv`)
- **Final Combined Research Dataset:** 3,000 records (`data/synthetic/loan_evaluation_dataset_3000.csv`)
- **Fitted Model Artifact:** `data/synthetic/sdv_model.pkl`
- **Metadata Specification:** `data/synthetic/sdv_metadata.json`

---

## 2. Academic Clarification & Data Provenance

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Academic Integrity & Data Nature Disclaimer:**
> The 2,500 augmented records are **synthetic records generated by learning statistical patterns from the 500 Google Form responses**. They do not represent real bank customers, nor do they reflect identifiable living individuals. In this research pipeline, we strictly distinguish between:
> 1. **Original Questionnaire Responses (500 records):** Primary empirical survey responses collected from participants via structured Google Form questionnaires.
> 2. **SDV-Generated Synthetic Records (2,500 records):** Statistically synthesized tabular observations generated by training a CTGAN model on the joint probability distribution of the 500 empirical responses.
> 3. **Combined Research Dataset (3,000 records):** The unified benchmark dataset comprising the 500 unchanged original responses (rows 0–499) followed by the 2,500 SDV synthetic records (rows 500–2999).

## 4. Synthesizer Configuration & Reproducibility Settings

The data generation process was executed under strict deterministic constraints to ensure exact experimental reproducibility across all academic evaluations:

- **SDV Framework Version:** `sdv==1.38.1`
- **Underlying GAN Architecture:** `CTGAN` (`ctgan==0.12.1`)
- **Deep Learning Framework:** `PyTorch 2.13.0`
- **Training Epochs:** 300
- **Random Seed:** `42` (configured across Python `random`, `numpy.random`, and `torch.manual_seed`)
- **Deterministic Flags:** `torch.backends.cudnn.deterministic = True`, `torch.backends.cudnn.benchmark = False`
- **Synthesizer Class:** `sdv.single_table.CTGANSynthesizer`
- **Metadata Specification:** Single-table V1 metadata with all 16 predictor columns and 1 target column designated with `sdtype="categorical"`.

## 8. Limitations & Methodological Constraints

1. **Empirical Seed Scale:** The seed dataset consists of $N=500$ survey records. While CTGAN effectively learns multivariate categorical correlations from this sample size, rare sub-combinations in the population may have wider confidence intervals.
2. **Tabular Synthesizer Specificity:** CTGAN approximates continuous latent representations for high-cardinality discrete categories. Minor frequency shifts (e.g., in `Marital Status` or `Existing Loan Status`) represent normal generative variation in adversarial training.
3. **Class Distribution:** The empirical survey contained 46.40% Approved and 53.60% Rejected. The synthesized dataset generated 32.52% Approved and 67.48% Rejected, resulting in a combined research dataset of 34.83% Approved ($N=1,045$) and 65.17% Rejected ($N=1,955$). This distribution will be balanced or stratified appropriately during downstream machine learning model training in subsequent steps.
